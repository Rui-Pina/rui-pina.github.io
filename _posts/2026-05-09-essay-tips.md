Best Essay-Writing Tips from https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jztZpqnDw8A

* Actually answer the question. Focus on the question, the whole question, and nothing but the question. Do not write generally around the topic.
* Treat an essay like an argument, not a brain dump. Every paragraph should help build your overall answer.
* Avoid “shopping list” essays. Do not simply list points one after another. Show how the ideas connect, like ingredients in a recipe.
* Plan before writing. Poor essays often feel like stream of consciousness. A clear plan helps the reader follow your argument.
* Read enough to command the material. Strong essays show that you understand the key texts, lectures, debates, and evidence.
* Use sources properly. Attribute other people’s ideas clearly. Do not plagiarise or rely on copied material.
* Build a clear structure. A good essay has a beginning, middle, and end, with each section contributing to the central argument.
* Analyze rather than just describe. Break the question into parts, define key terms, examine assumptions, and reconstruct a focused answer.
* Pay attention to every part of the question. For example, if the question asks “why might autocrats grant power to a wider electorate?”, examine terms like why, autocrats, power, grant, and wider electorate.
* Show independent judgment. The best essays do not just summarize what others have said; they explain what you think and why.
* Add value to the debate. Try to contribute something fresh, whether through your argument, definitions, method, evidence, or case studies.
* Use “I argue” where appropriate. In some academic contexts, saying “I argue…” can make your own contribution clearer.
* Think beyond theory. You can contribute at different levels:
    * concepts: how key terms are defined
    * theory: the explanation or argument
    * meta-theory: how broadly the theory applies
    * methodology: how ideas are tested
    * evidence: examples, data, or case studies
* Write clearly and elegantly. Academic writing (also make it so the populus can read it and understand it clearly) should not be unnecessarily obscure. Make the reader want to keep reading.
* Make the essay interesting. The strongest essays are not only accurate; they are engaging, fresh, and enjoyable to read.
* Do not rely on AI or shortcuts. The point of an essay is to try for yourself, not to compile or outsource the thinking.
* Remember the purpose of an essay: to try. A great essay shows your own serious attempt to understand, analyze, and contribute.
