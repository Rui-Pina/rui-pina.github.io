Tolkien enjoys himself with the rhythms and sounds of these poems. He moves between comic songs, strange creatures and more sombre verses, presenting them as part of the storytelling culture around the Shire. That sense of Hobbits having their own songs and jokes is a charming addition to Middle-earth.

Despite the title, this is a collection of poems rather than a sustained adventure about Tom Bombadil. Some are delightful to read aloud; others depend on a taste for nonsense verse. Pleasant, inventive and worth reading for Tolkien's verse, though less absorbing than his longer fiction.
