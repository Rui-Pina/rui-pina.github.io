Um Grande Projeto Nacional presents Enéas Carneiro’s vision for Brazil’s economic development, national sovereignty, and industrial policy. Written in his direct and uncompromising style, the book argues for a stronger state role in strategic sectors, investment in science and technology, and the pursuit of greater national independence. Really formative.
