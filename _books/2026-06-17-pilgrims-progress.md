The Pilgrim’s Progress is one of the most influential works of Christian literature ever written. If I am not mistaken is the first usage of "Christian allegory". The allegorical journey of its protagonist, Christian, John Bunyan explores themes of faith, perseverance, temptation, and redemption in a way that has resonated with readers for centuries.

Bunyan’s use of vivid symbolism and memorable characters gives the story a timeless quality. While the language reflects its seventeenth-century origins, the central message remains accessible, and many of the moral and spiritual struggles depicted feel surprisingly universal. Readers with an interest in theology, literature, or the history of Christian thought will like it.
