Walton’s prose is elegant and often charming, offering valuable insight into the religious and cultural world of early modern England. However, modern readers may find that the biographies lean more toward reverent portraiture than critical scholarship. The pacing can feel slow, and some anecdotes are included more for their moral or spiritual significance than their historical reliability.
